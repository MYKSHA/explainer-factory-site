Who uses this app

Explainer Factory is used only by its owner to manage the owner's own YouTube channels. No other people sign in to it.

What data the app accesses

With the owner's permission through Google sign-in, the app uses these Google scopes:

  • youtube.upload and youtube.force-ssl: to upload videos, set metadata, thumbnails, playlists and publish schedules on the owner's channels.
  • yt-analytics.readonly: to read views, watch time and subscriber statistics for the owner's channels.

How data is stored

OAuth tokens and downloaded statistics are stored only on the owner's own computer. Nothing is sent to any server other than Google's APIs. The app does not collect, sell or share personal data of any viewer or third party.

Use of Google user data

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. See also the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Local tokens are then deleted.
